Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

SovereignTapes ("we", "us") is a music platform for albums, EPs, singles, mixtapes, music videos and artist stories, available at sovereigntapes.com and in our Android, iPhone and Windows apps. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider), display name, @username, and whether you are a fan or an artist.
  • Profile content you add: profile photo, cover image, bio, show dates and booking details.
  • Content you upload: music, cover art, music videos, YouTube links, and editorial stories, plus comments, ratings, likes and follows.
  • Listening activity: which releases are streamed or downloaded and when, used for charts, artist analytics and download limits.
  • Security data: if you turn on two-step sign-in, your authenticator setup and one-time backup codes (stored only as hashes).
  • Payments: purchases, tips and subscriptions are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We keep a record of what was bought and paid out.
  • City requests: if you tap "I'd go" to ask an artist to perform near you, the city you enter.

We do not use the advertising ID, we do not show third-party ads, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To run your account, show your profile and releases, and keep the service secure.
  • To pay artists and DJs, and to process purchases and subscriptions.
  • To build charts, show artists their own listener analytics, and enforce download limits.
  • To send account emails such as sign-in codes and password resets.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Stripe: processes payments and artist payouts.
  • Brevo: sends our account emails.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • YouTube: when you play a YouTube video an artist linked, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that video.

Public content (your display name, @username, profile, releases, stories, comments and ratings) can be seen by anyone. Your email address is never shown publicly.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time: open SovereignTapes (app or sovereigntapes.com/#account), sign in, go to Account, and use Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, profile, uploads, stories, likes, follows and comments. Payment records may be kept where the law requires it. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Keeping your data safe

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access is limited by database rules so people can only change their own content. Two-step sign-in is available for every account.

Children

SovereignTapes is not directed at children under 13, and some music on the platform is explicit.
